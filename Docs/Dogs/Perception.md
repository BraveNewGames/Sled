# Perception and threat responses

## Approved

None yet.

## Proposed

Explore how dogs notice environmental events, react to uncertainty or danger, and alert the player or other dogs.

## Open questions

- Which senses and stimuli should the first prototype represent?
- How should a dog communicate uncertainty before a threat is confirmed?
- How do personality, trust, and group behavior affect responses?

## Dependencies

Personality, emotions, social behavior, and autonomous behavior.
